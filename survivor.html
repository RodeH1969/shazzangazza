How it works
One masked photo. Every 10 minutes.
  • A new masked celebrity photo drops every 10 minutes.
  • You've got 3 minutes to submit your guess.
  • Results and the correct answer are published 9 minutes in.
  • Only correct guessers move on to the next round.
  • Last one standing wins a $40 Aldi gift card.
  • If nobody's left after a round, nobody wins that round.
